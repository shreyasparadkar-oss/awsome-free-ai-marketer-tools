## Content Writing & Copywriting
* **ChatGPT** - The gold standard for drafting blogs, brainstorming ad angles, and rewriting copy. (Free tier available)
* **Claude AI** - Excellent for writing long-form marketing content with a highly natural, human tone. (Free tier available)
* **Copy.ai** - Provides quick, dedicated templates for social captions, email subject lines, and ad headlines. (Free tier available)

## Image Generation & Editing
* **Canva AI** - Built-in magic design tools to generate, expand, or erase elements in social media graphics instantly. (Free tier available)
* **Adobe Firefly** - High-quality generative AI for commercial-safe marketing images and text effects. (Free credits monthly)
* **Photoroom** - Flawlessly removes and replaces backgrounds for e-commerce product photos using AI. (Free tier available)

## Video Production & AI Avatars
* **CapCut AI** - Generates auto-captions, scripts, and video edits perfect for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. (Free app/web tool)
* **HeyGen** - Create interactive, talking AI avatars from plain text scripts. (Free trial credits)
* **Lumen5** - Turns your written blog posts into engaging marketing videos automatically. (Free tier available)

## SEO & Keyword Research
* **AnswerThePublic** - Discovers the exact questions people ask on Google to help map out your content marketing. (Free daily searches)
* **Perplexity AI** - Real-time web searching AI that helps track competitor strategies and current marketing trends. (Free tier available)

## Social Media Management
* **ManyChat** - Automates Instagram and Facebook comment replies to drive leads straight to your DMs. (Free tier available)
* **Headline Analyzer (CoSchedule)** - Uses data science to score your titles and emotional hooks for maximum click-through rates. (Free tool)
